How this stays honest: the internal clocks derive from a real cadence (the league resets Thursdays 14:00 UTC, which is Friday 00:00 in Hobart) — and a cadence that has been paused says so and stops counting, rather than ticking down to a date nothing is keeping; the milestone clocks are wired to pdoom-data, cleaned and human-verified, and show “awaiting data” rather than a made-up date until that lands. The AGI clock waits on the game's own calibration. Colours are pulled from the game's shipped palette.
