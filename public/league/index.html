This page is retired, and everything on it predates ladder epoch L3

The weekly league is not running. This page is kept reachable but is not linked from anywhere on the site and is excluded from search engines — hidden, not deleted, so the record survives.

Nothing shown below is a competition result. Until 30 July 2026 the rollover ran every Sunday at 14:00 UTC on a Monday-to-Sunday week — two days out of phase with the game's own Friday cadence — and it opened the week that was ending ten hours later rather than the week that was starting, so week numbers and date ranges here are one week behind what they describe. No shipped game client ever submitted a score to these board keys. The seed shown below was derived by this website, not sent by the game; the seed that scores are actually submitted under is recorded in the league seed ledger, and it is not this one.

The league week now runs Friday 00:00 to Thursday 23:59 in Hobart, and the record starts fresh with the week beginning Friday 31 July 2026 — the L2 → L3 ladder fork. L3 removed the action-point pool in favour of an attention economy and changed enough else that scores set under the old rules are not measuring the same game. Boards are identified by (seed, L3) — the ladder epoch, never the build number — so a patch release can no longer split a board in half.

The full account, with file paths and counts, is in docs/LEAGUE_EPOCH_ANOMALY.md. Past weeks are listed, and labelled, on the archive page.
