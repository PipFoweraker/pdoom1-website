[ANOMALY] Pre-history: weeks before 7 August 2026

The — weeks below were produced before the epoch fork on Friday 7 August 2026. They are kept and shown deliberately, but they are not results and are not comparable to anything after that date.

The cut is a fork, not a tidy date. 7 August is the first Friday of the month, which is when the game's rules version and its ladder version both change — so scores either side of it were set under different rules and cannot be ranked against each other. (31 July, the previous Friday, only refreshed the board on unchanged rules, which is why the cut is not there.)

What actually happened before it: the weekly rollover ran every Sunday at 14:00 UTC on a Monday-to-Sunday week, two days out of phase with the game's own Friday cadence, and it created the week that was ending ten hours later instead of the week that was starting — so each record is labelled one week behind the period it describes. Every one of these weeks also contains zero entries — no shipped game client ever submitted a score to these board keys — and the seeds shown were derived by the website rather than sent by the game. Some records still carry v0.4.1 version stamps from the old pygame client; those are left exactly as found, because restamping them would invent a history that did not happen.

From 30 July 2026 the league week runs Friday 00:00 to Thursday 23:59 in Hobart, which is the clock everything on this site is now anchored to.

They are retained for the record, not for competition. Each card lists the specific problems recorded against that week; the full account, with file paths and counts, is in docs/LEAGUE_EPOCH_ANOMALY.md.
