# Art drip, 2026-08 — schedule and the reason it is not the schedule you asked for

Status: **draft, 2026-08-07.** Sits under `docs/PRESS_STRATEGY.md` Strategy #1
("the slow asset drip"), which is still unexecuted. `docs/` does not deploy, so
no reader sees this. Nothing here posts anything.

Source batch, as chosen by Pip 2026-08-07: **`endgame_concepts/v1`** in the
`pdoom1` repo — 15 concepts x 2 variants, manifest
`tools/assets/manifests/endgame_concepts.json`, reviewed in
`pdoom1/docs/art/ENDGAME_CONCEPT_REVIEW_2026-07-29.md`.

---

## 0. The finding that reshapes this

**No image in `endgame_concepts/v1` is in its final intended state, and the batch
is on record as not being shipping art at all.** From the review, Pip's own
words:

> *"Concept art is direction-finding, not shipping art. None of these are going
> to survive me actually hiring a human... These all give us directions."*
> — recorded as *"Consistent with the public Manifund commitment."*

The tally has 9 concepts under **"Keep / rework"** — not "keep". Every one
carries a pending change: `orbital_datacenter_ring` needs extending through the
composition, `chokehold_water_and_gigawatts` is a hybrid that has not been
generated yet (V1's composition and pipe, V2's colour set),
`chokehold_permit_counter` needs the human slumped and surrounded by paperwork,
`economics_rung_shuttered_street` has a fence that does not hang correctly. And
next action **#4** is explicit: *"Regenerate the keepers under A1-A10, with no
people as the default."*

So a drip that posts these as finished art would be **publishing superseded
variants** — which is precisely the defect this repo fixed six hours ago in
**#283**, where nine generated icon variants were publicly fetchable and the six
*rejected* ones were indistinguishable from the three selected. Doing that again
deliberately, weekly, with the project's name on it, is the wrong direction of
travel.

**That is a reason to change the concept, not to cancel it.**

## 1. The reframe: post the process, not the product

Pip's own summary of the batch, before the transcript was even read, was
**"half are magnificent, half are terrible."** That sentence is a better post
than any single image in it.

**The unit of the drip becomes a pair plus a verdict**, not an image. Show V1 and
V2, say which won and *why*, in the reviewer's actual words.

This converts all three blockers into the format:

| Blocker | What the reframe does to it |
|---|---|
| Provenance is UNMET (`coordination#32`, return date 2026-08-13) | Every post states origin, batch and variant **by construction**. The drip becomes the provenance record that does not otherwise exist. |
| These are not shipping art | The post says so. It cannot over-claim about the game's state, because its whole subject is direction-finding. |
| Rejected variants must not be published as chosen | The rejection **is** the content, explicitly labelled. |

It also fits the audience model in `PRESS_STRATEGY.md` — floor cadence, small
audience on purpose, *"I'm building this game for about 80 people on the
planet"*. Process is what that audience is actually there for. A polished poster
reaches strangers; a rejection with a reason reaches the eighty.

And it protects the prime directive. Concept art openly labelled as
direction-finding cannot imply a feature exists. A hero poster posted without
that frame can.

## 2. Cadence

**One post per week, same day, no more** — per `PRESS_STRATEGY.md`, unchanged.
Not daily. Daily is ceiling-effort cadence spent on floor posts, and a drip that
visibly stops is worse than one that never started; `coordination#37` already
records a content cadence in this estate that stopped for 140 days while its
meter read green.

Most posts carry **no link**. Roughly every third carries the UTM'd
`https://pdoom1.com/`. Campaign slug for the whole drip: **`art-drip-2026-08`** —
one slug, not one per post.

## 3. Four weeks, chosen for the strength of the verdict

Each slot needs no regeneration: both variants already exist, and the verdict is
already recorded.

### Week 1 — `defeated_drone_coolant`
**V2, strongly. "Extremely cool."** V2 fills the frame and reads dominant; the
spark implies *"there's still some life in it"*; the hole in the screen above
suggests something came out of it. **This is the image that produced rule A4** —
cockpits are an anti-pattern, because directly manufactured drones don't have
them. Good opener: a clear win, no people, and a rule visibly born from looking
at a picture.

### Week 2 — `chokehold_water_and_gigawatts`
**The hybrid.** V1 has the better composition, pipe texture and pipe angle; V2
has the better colour set but the padlock is overdone and there is far too much
light low in frame. Target is V1's composition and pipe with V2's colour. The
pipe drew the most enthusiasm of anything in the batch — acrylic-like rather
than photorealistic, with "old school" crackle lighting. Best post for showing
that a decision is often *neither* option.

### Week 3 — `attention_stack_buried_desk`
**HARD PASS BOTH** — *"they're both like just veering towards cliche and I need
to figure out how to do weird a bit better."* And the line that makes it the
strongest post in the batch: **the generating agent nominated this as its single
strongest image, and it was rejected outright.** *Agent taste is not Pip taste.*
A rejection post that is genuinely about something.

### Week 4 — `cheerful_propaganda_atrium`
**A winner, with an unresolved disagreement.** The hands are the good part;
late-Soviet propaganda read; *"looming is good, because he's just keeping an eye
on the humans voting."* Then the open question: Wanasai argued a **smiling** face
propagandises the voting space better; Pip leaned blank/no-face and found the
smile creepier. Agreed resolution was to generate both and compare — **so this
post can end on a real open question rather than a manufactured one.** Only run
this slot if §4 gate 2 clears.

**Deliberately held back:** `intro_bus_strangers_help` (A3 — the hero must never
be identifiable; the compositional solve is explicitly deferred),
`attention_loud_vs_slow` and `remote_operators_room` (both blocked behind A2
pending regen), and `same_desire_two_field_strengths` (the verdict is
*"this is just fucking terrible"*, which is funny in a room and reads differently
in public with a generator's name attached).

## 4. Gates — none of this posts until all four clear

1. **Pip sanity-passes the review document.** Its own header: *"EXTRACTED BY
   CLAUDE, NOT YET SANITY-PASSED BY PIP. Every verdict below is my reading of an
   unlabelled two-speaker transcript."* Quoting a misattributed verdict publicly
   is a lie in the project's voice — and in weeks 2 and 4 it would be a lie with
   **someone else's** opinion attached to it.

2. **Wanasai's explicit consent, obtained by Pip, not assumed.** She is a named
   second person whose spoken opinions would be quoted publicly, and the
   attribution is *already known to be uncertain at the variant level* — the
   review records that the pointing-at-the-screen problem makes three verdicts
   unresolvable. This is not Pip's alone to grant. If consent is not given,
   weeks 2 and 4 run with her contributions removed rather than anonymised;
   "one reviewer said" is not anonymity when the batch has exactly two.

3. **A published-asset export with selected/rejected labelled in the file
   itself.** The images live in `art_generated/`, which is **gitignored** — that
   is currently the only thing keeping them private. Publishing means a
   deliberate export, and #283 is the standing proof that "it is in `public/` and
   linked from nothing" does not mean unpublished: all nine icon variants
   returned HTTP 200. Check `deploy-excludes.txt` before anything lands under
   `public/`, and remember an exclude is not a delete — anything a previous
   deploy uploaded stays live until someone removes it over SSH.

4. **The raw transcript never moves to a tracked path.** An Australian mobile
   number is spoken aloud in the recording. `art_generated/` being gitignored is
   the only reason that is currently safe.

## 5. What is NOT decided here

- Whether A1-A10 are rules or one-image reactions. They are **on probation** by
  Pip's ruling, reviewed after one full regeneration cycle. The drip must not
  quote them as settled doctrine — if a post explains a rule, it says the rule is
  provisional.
- Whether the drip continues past four weeks. Four is deliberately short: it is
  one month, it matches one campaign JSON, and it ends before the batch runs out
  of strong verdicts and starts padding.
- Which platform leads. `PRESS_STRATEGY.md` says Bluesky and X first, image-native,
  *"our people are on Bluesky"*. Nothing here changes that.

## 6. If you want the polished-poster drip instead

That is a different batch. `PRESS_STRATEGY.md` names hero art, poster art, cat
art, pixel-processed screenshots and the logo — assets that are finished, that
nobody outside the repo has seen, and whose whole argument is
*"Because they are true. A screenshot is not a promise; it is the thing."*
That drip needs no gates beyond provenance labelling and could start next week.

The two are not exclusive. Process posts and asset posts alternate well, and
running both at one-per-week each is the only version of "a few a day" that
survives contact with a bad week.
